Everything a disciplined trader needs

Log in seconds

Copy your last trade, tap a symbol, and type a stop by distance. Typo checks warn you before a wrong price is saved.

Plan first, close later

Save your entry, stop and target when you take the trade. Add the exit when it closes.

Find your repeat mistakes

Tag mistakes on each trade and get a weekly review of overtrading, missing stops and revenge sizing.

Bulls Score

One score from win rate, profit factor, recovery, consistency and discipline, with a radar chart that shows your weak spot.

Risk and lot size

Set 2 to 3 percent risk and get the lot size for Gold and forex, from a $100 account to $100k.

News that moves Gold

A high-impact calendar for USD events like NFP, CPI and FOMC, plus XAUUSD headlines.

Win rate by day, hour, session

See when you trade best and when you should stay out.

Import from MT4 and MT5

Bring in your broker history with exact entries, exits and profit.

Reminders and daily quotes

Phone alarms for your routine and a fresh discipline quote every day.

How it works

Create your account

Sign up with your email. Your trades stay private to you.

Log and tag

Record each trade, attach your screenshots and tag any mistakes honestly.

Review every week

Open Insights, read your findings and fix one thing next week.

Install it on your phone

Android

Open the app in Chrome or Brave, tap the menu, then Install app or Add to Home screen.

iPhone

Open it in Safari, tap Share, then Add to Home Screen.
